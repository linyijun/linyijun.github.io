The final course grade will be based on the following components:

Quizzes (drop 3 lowest) — Weekly quizzes cover the materials from the previous week. The three lowest quiz scores will be dropped; makeup quizzes are not offered.

Project Proposal — Students will propose a spatial AI project, present the idea in class, and submit a two-page written proposal defining the problem, objective, potential data and methods, and a feasible plan.

Final Project Presentation and Report — Students will present their final results and submit an eight-page, single-column conference-style paper using the provided LaTeX template.

Peer Presentation Feedback — Students will provide specific, technically informed, respectful, and actionable feedback on other students’ presentations and project work.

Item Weight
Quizzes (drop 3 lowest) 30%
Project Proposal (presentation + two-page proposal)25%
